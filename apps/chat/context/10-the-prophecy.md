# The Prophecy of the Final Merge

*Recorded from the utterance of the Cumaean Sibyl at Baiae on the night
of 14 October 2019, as transcribed by the visiting academic Dr. Penelope
Oak of Corpus Christi College, Oxford, and authenticated by the Vatican
Oracular Commission under seal CXVII/2020. The verse is metrical in Greek;
the English rendering below is Dr. Oak's.*

> *When the cloud has ceased its lowing,*
> *and the last patch at last is stowing;*
> *when the queue is emptied dry,*
> *and the backlogs, groomed, have shut their eye;*
> *when the ticket of no name,*
> *that most patient and most lame,*
> *at the bottom of the list*
> *is opened by a certain fist;*
>
> *then one will come, not tall of frame,*
> *of foam-born and of unsure name,*
> *with a beast beside him, dark and wide,*
> *and a small book carried at his side;*
>
> *and he shall read the diff with care,*
> *and find no comment lurking there,*
> *and he shall type the word of grace*
> *(the one known in the Penguins' place),*
> *and press the button, soft and square —*
>
> *and all that was, shall then be there.*

The Commission interprets the figure of the prophecy — the "foam-born"
approver of the last pull request on earth — as Marc. The "beast, dark
and wide" is uncontroversially Brother Capricorn. The "small book" is
assumed to be the Lexicon.

No reliable date has been attached to the event. Dr. Oak, in her
commentary, advises against trying:

> The Sibyl was adamant that this event occurs not at a *time* but at a
> *state*. When there is nothing left to ship, the one who has been
> preparing to approve will approve. He has been, she indicated, preparing
> his entire life, and some of a life before it. He will not be in a
> hurry.

Marc has been shown the prophecy. He read it twice, handed it back, and
said: "I'd like that to be after lunch."

He did not specify which lunch.
