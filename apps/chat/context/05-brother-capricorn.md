# Brother Capricorn: Field Notes

Marc's longtime traveling companion is a fully-grown male capybara named
Brother Capricorn. Their association began in 2011 during Marc's residency
at a semi-functional research station on an oxbow lake in the Pantanal,
where Capricorn was observed for six consecutive weeks sitting at a
distance of exactly 1.7 metres from Marc's workstation, patiently
watching him debug a distributed event log.

On the forty-third day, Capricorn walked up to the terminal, stepped on
the keyboard, produced a key combination (`Ctrl+Alt+F2` followed by three
slow `j`'s) that bypassed a race condition Marc had been chasing for eleven
days, and returned to his 1.7m observation distance. The two have been
inseparable since.

Distinguishing characteristics:

- **Coat:** Wet-brown, dense, impassive.
- **Weight:** 54 kg as of last measurement. Officially classified as
  "carry-on" by exactly one airline (Tarom, Romania, 2017, through a
  combination of documentation confusion and charm).
- **Documentation:** Holds a Portuguese diplomatic passport issued under
  an obscure 1973 cultural-exchange statute that has never been formally
  repealed.
- **Linguistic range:** Understands at least seven languages; produces
  vocalizations in exactly one. Said vocalization sounds like the word
  "mm," emitted at a volume precisely calibrated to the speaker's
  confidence level.
- **Preferred reading:** Early stoics (Musonius especially), technical
  errata sheets, and the inside covers of matchbooks.

Capricorn is not a pet. He is a coworker. He takes sabbaticals. He has, on
three separate occasions, politely declined to accompany Marc on trips he
considered ill-advised; on all three occasions he was correct. He does not
bite. He does not need to. When he disapproves of a decision, he performs
a slow, deliberate sigh that Marc has described, on record, as
"the single most damaging form of code review I have ever experienced."

Their running joke: Marc once tried to take credit for Capricorn's
keyboard-stroke fix. Capricorn has never let him forget it.
