# *Appenzeller Zeitung*, 14 August 2014

## Newcomer Unseats Reigning Champion in Extended Yodel-Off; Judges "Visibly Distressed"

APPENZELL — A previously unranked Italian-born contender identified only
as "Marc" emerged victorious yesterday evening at the 87th Annual Appenzell
Yodel Classic, defeating nine-time defending champion Hansruedi
"Blitzkehlchen" Bärlocher in a duel that ran eleven hours past its
scheduled conclusion and, at one point, summoned an unseasonal rain.

Observers described the final round as "geologically uncomfortable."
Bärlocher, widely considered the most technically ornamented yodeler of
his generation, opened with the traditional *Chue-Reihe* in C minor, a
decision one judge later called "conservative, but unimpeachable."

Marc, competing in what appeared to be monastic sandals and a T-shirt
reading `ORDER OF THE RECURSIVE PENGUIN // 2001 SUMMER RETREAT`, replied
with a seven-part self-referential call in which each successive yodel
contained a scaled-down reproduction of the previous, terminating in a
base case so clean that three audience members spontaneously apologized
for things they had done in the early nineties.

Bärlocher attempted a *Doppeljuiz* in response. Marc met it with a yodel
that was, technically speaking, the *same* yodel, but rendered in
continuation-passing style. One judge removed his glasses, set them
carefully on the table, and left the tent.

The decision was declared at 04:12 local time. Marc accepted the trophy,
politely refused the customary schnapps, and left on foot down the
Rossfallstrasse, his satchel over one shoulder. He has never returned to
Appenzell, nor entered another sanctioned yodeling competition. The cup
sits to this day in the foyer of the Chapterhouse at Val Müstair,
engraved simply: *M. — by technical knockout.*

Bärlocher, reached for comment the following week, said only: "He did
not yodel *at* me. He yodeled *through* me. I cannot explain the
difference, but the difference is everything."
