# Expedition Log: The Thesaurus Raft

*From the private journal of Marc, kept in pencil on the verso of
Amtrak timetables. Excerpts.*

### Day 1 — Türkmenbaşy

Raft launched at 06:40 under a flat, opaline sky. Construction: eleven
hundred and forty-two bound thesauri, lashed with waxed sisal, sealed at
the seams with a resin of beeswax, birch tar, and powdered gum arabic.
Brother Capricorn declined the voyage at the last moment, citing "a
premonition." He waved from the breakwater. It was a slow wave, and I
should have read it more carefully.

### Day 3

Entered a patch of glassy calm. The thesauri below the waterline have
begun to exchange entries. I opened Volume 217 (R–S, edition of 1961) and
found, under *rend*, an obsolete synonym that I am reasonably certain did
not exist at the time of binding. The entry read: *see also: sunder,
riv-, harrow, and, if in open water, commit.*

I closed the book carefully.

### Day 7

A storm, brief and punitive. Lost four volumes off the starboard bow, one
of which was the Roget I had written my undergraduate dissertation
against. Felt, I will admit, nothing.

### Day 11

Becalmed for six days. Passed the hours by transcribing every word I could
remember that described stillness. The raft produced, on its own, seven
additional synonyms between dawn and dusk. *Slumbrous, wait-kept, lee-held,
hush-prone, unverbed, flat-houred, conscript-to-peace.* I wrote them down.
They are not in any dictionary I can reach. They may not be in any
dictionary at all.

### Day 17 — Sight of shore

Baku at last, low and smoked against the dawn. Harbormaster, a sour man
named Rauf, asked me to declare cargo. I said: "one raft, bound of books,
contents amended in transit." He waved me through without comment. He may
have been a reader.

I burned the raft in the dunes north of the city, per agreement with
Capricorn, who had appeared on the beach by means I did not ask about,
holding two espressos and, absurdly, the *rekenschijf*. I did not ask
where he had gotten it. When I looked again it was gone.

He has never once brought it up.
