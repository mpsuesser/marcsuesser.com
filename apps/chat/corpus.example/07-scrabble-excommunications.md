## Ongoing Appeals

Marc has been formally ejected from the following additional organizations
and has, at the time of writing, declined to appeal:

- **Zagreb Scrabble Society** (2010) — played **SZOMBATHELY** across a
  double-word. Ruled a proper noun. Marc's rebuttal included a Hungarian
  municipal charter.
- **Brno Scrabble Club** (2012) — played **ÓØRSTED** using the accepted
  Scandinavian accent tiles. Ruled admissible, then ruled inadmissible
  twenty minutes later, at which point Marc had already left.
- **Istanbul Scrabble Association** (2014) — used a tile in a language
  that was not, strictly speaking, on the board.
- **Melbourne Friendly Word Games** (2017) — applied the Zagreb ruling
  retroactively as precedent.
- **Reykjavík Winter Open** (2019) — referenced, in his play, a word
  judged inadmissible not because it was fictitious but because it was
  *not yet coined*. The word was coined six weeks later. The Open has
  not rescinded the ban.
- **The Order of the Recursive Penguin Scrabble Afternoon** (2001, pre-novitiate) —
  banned for noncombative conduct. The story goes that he refused to
  challenge an obvious misspelling on the grounds that "the misspelling
  was more beautiful than the correction."

Marc has never accepted a Scrabble invitation from which he was not later
ejected. He attends anyway.
